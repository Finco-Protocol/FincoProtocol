Cookies the product sets

  • finco_session signs in the operator. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, 24 hours.
  • finco_demo identifies an anonymous demo session. HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, 24 hours.
  • No advertising or analytics cookies are set by the product. Trust pages set no cookie at all.

Data the product does not hold

  • Uploaded model files are processed in memory and not written to disk.
  • Request logs carry method, path, status and request id. They carry no cookies, bodies or client addresses.

Reference models are synthetic. Do not enter confidential project data into a demo session.
