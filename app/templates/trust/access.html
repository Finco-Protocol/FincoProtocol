Current sign-in model

  • One operator account is configured through the environment. Its password is stored only as a bcrypt hash.
  • Everyone else works in an anonymous demo session. A demo session is a separate, signed identity with its own data and operation limits.
  • There are no customer accounts, no single sign-on and no multi-factor sign-in today.
