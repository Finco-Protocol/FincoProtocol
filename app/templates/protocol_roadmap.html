Terminal & On-chain Foundation

Model track · Institutional Modelling Turn project-finance calculations into a decision-ready modelling product.

The commercial value is not “more formulas”. It is reproducible runs, controlled scenarios, deeper project-finance workflows and outputs that can move through investment-committee, lender and adviser review.

Institutional Model Runs

Explicit assumption state, run identity and traceable outputs so a decision can be reproduced later.

Advanced Project Finance

Deeper debt sizing, coverage, sponsor funding, reserves and waterfall workflows surfaced around the core engine.

Scenario Control

Institutional Reporting

Decision-ready exports with calculation and evidence lineage for investment-committee, lender and adviser workflows.
