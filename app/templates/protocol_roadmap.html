FINCO Protocol / Roadmap

From financial modelling to financial intelligence infrastructure.

FINCO is organized around four primary products — Model, Radar, Yield and Crypto — with Verify, API and $FINCO as the protocol layer. Roadmap status is separated into what is live or implemented now, Q4 2026 targets, Q1 2027 targets and later work.

Product principle AI explains. FINCO calculates.

New interfaces may change quickly. Calculation authority, evidence boundaries and reproducible outputs do not.

Product architecture

MODEL · RADAR · YIELD · CRYPTO

Primary products

Protocol layer: VERIFY · API · $FINCO. Verified Assets is a Verify composition surface, not a fifth primary product.

LIVE / IMPLEMENTED

What exists today.

Shipped

The shipped/implemented baseline includes Model, Radar, Verify/Verified Assets, API, the read-only Yield product and the wallet/entitlement foundation. “Implemented” does not mean every feature is production-active: Yield execution remains OFF, external alert delivery is not shipped, and production $FINCO activation is not configured.
