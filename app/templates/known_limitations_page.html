Model Scope & Limitations

Current FINCO Model coverage and boundaries for the synthetic reference models and working models.

Synthetic reference models

Generic Solar Reference, Generic Wind Reference and Generic Data Center Reference are the live production synthetic models covered by the public regression ring. They are not representations of real projects, companies or jurisdictions.

Generic Storage Reference is also synthetic and protected, but its runtime validation coverage is currently narrower than Solar/Wind/Data Center. Storage working-copy runtime is not released.

EV Charging is a production vertical (V1). Known V1 limitations: charging points are display metadata only; payment/B.11 fees are a fixed amount rather than dynamically percentage-linked; availability is informational because equivalent full-load hours are net of availability.

Methodology boundaries

  • Debt sizing. The engine supports deterministic debt sizing and coverage analysis, but specialist lender-specific structures may require additional modelling.
  • Tax. Country-specific tax conclusions require an explicit reviewed policy configuration; generic market profiles are illustrative.
  • Storage. Storage modelling is available as a synthetic reference surface, with narrower runtime and validation coverage than Solar/Wind.
  • Portfolio. Portfolio capabilities remain experimental relative to the core single-project engine.

Not currently covered

  • Full multi-lender syndication and complex intercreditor structures.
  • All jurisdiction-specific tax, legal or accounting interpretations.
  • External audit, credit approval or investment recommendation.

Disclaimer: Outputs are financial modelling estimates and do not constitute legal, tax, accounting, credit or investment advice.
