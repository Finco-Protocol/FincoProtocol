## Workstream B / D handoff contracts

### B: monthly CAPEX and construction profile

- B owns cost amounts/writers and profile editing, not operating frequency.
- Proposed shared interface remains the existing code-keyed CAPEX amounts plus
  `ConstructionCapexTimingInput.payment_weights` on typed monthly dates. Amounts
  do not move into timing metadata. No duplicate per-quarter CAPEX vector.
- Profile length must match its construction axis; normalized weights and sums
  must retain existing validation. Inactive periods cannot absorb positive cost.
- Finance costs, facility tails and VAT lag retain their own dated dimensions.
- Future integration needs complete monthly-to-model reporting membership with
  exact date endpoints. Construction inclusive days must not be silently treated
  as operating half-open days. Never solve the mismatch by dropping a month.
- Required acknowledgement: profile schema/dates, partial first/last months,
  amount/hash ownership and whether any proposed change alters financial timing.

### D: OPEX period requirements

- D owns its editor/annual budgets and must not implement a second period engine.
- Retain annual amount, currency/unit, index/step clock, external-series year
  coverage, activity windows and amount-vs-settlement distinction.
- Annual financial-model year and calendar/tax year must be explicitly identified;
  quarter support does not authorize another annual escalation every two rows.
- Future integration requires a budget-to-operating-interval map and independent
  tests for stubs, leap years, one-off costs and Senior-tenor-linked expiry.
- Required acknowledgement: which paths consume `year_index`/`day_fraction`,
  whether cash timing is actually represented, and unsupported combinations.

## Financial release and scheduling constraints

The calendar, flow, debt, tax, stock and return rows marked F form ONE coherent
financial admission boundary, even if implemented as reviewed internal stages.
Do not ship a frequency setting after editing only PeriodEngine. Obtain exact
path scope, independent content review, additive successor pins and negative
governance tests; preserve all historical authorities. Then the P rows establish
authorized product admission and immutable evidence before any user activation.

The 3/6 and 6/3 modes require M decisions, not mere parameterization. Keep monthly
construction unchanged in all phases. Default stays released 6/6. No existing
numerical tolerance or golden is replaced by this proposal.
