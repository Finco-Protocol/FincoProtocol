# FINCO Protocol Roadmap

## Now

- FINCO Model: deterministic modelling, scenarios, reporting and synthetic references.
- FINCO Radar: reference-aware intelligence for tokenized markets.
- Public-readiness: clean repository, generic references, isolated demo sessions and load testing.

## Next

- evidence layer and deterministic signals,
- cross-market reference reconciliation,
- execution-aware quote and liquidity analysis,
- model integrity / audit diagnostics,
- richer portfolio intelligence.

## Later

- live financial digital twins,
- autonomous model-maintenance agents,
- capital-structure and refinancing optimization,
- global relative-value terminal,
- machine-readable due diligence and asset knowledge graph.

## Research

- cryptographic proof-of-model and proof-of-valuation,
- zero-knowledge financial proofs,
- on-chain financial attestations,
- counterfactual market simulation,
- autonomous capital-allocation research.

Research items are exploratory and are not delivery commitments.
