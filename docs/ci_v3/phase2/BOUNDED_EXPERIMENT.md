# Proposed first hosted comparison — no execution authorized

Request one control/shadow pair on one immutable, independently approved tested tree. The historical three-repeat suggestion is superseded: no automatic repeats or retries. Independently bind repository, HEAD/tree, exact-head or byte-identical synthetic merge proof, workflow/run/attempt/job identity, inventory/policy, evaluator bootstrap revision, resolved environment and service artifact identifiers. A worker cannot approve any of these inputs. Pin the actual downloaded bytes to independently obtained immutable artifact metadata; reject missing, substituted, expired or cross-attempt transfers.

The sequential Public Safety gate stays mandatory. The separate shadow graph is authorization admission → trusted full collection/whole-module plan → three concurrent workers → independent advisory aggregation. Admission and collection failure prevent worker launch; worker failure does not cancel siblings (`fail-fast: false`) so evidence can be collected. Failed, timed-out, cancelled or absent evidence fails the shadow conclusion. Never reuse a successful sibling from a different attempt.

Historical predictions, not Phase 2 performance results: the slow cohort predicted 32.78 minutes for three workers, with a 34–45 minute planning range and 99.71 aggregate worker minutes. Its sequential control was 95.98 minutes: approximately 195.69 runner-minutes for the pair's pytest work. The faster cohort predicted 19.99 minutes. Allow roughly 196–221 runner-minutes for pytest planning, plus separately measured trusted discovery, artifact transport and policy jobs. The old audit's roughly 11.9 policy runner-minutes are contextual, not a new measurement. Wall time is dominated by the control when both launch together; startup, queueing and transfer remain additional and unmeasured.

Proposed maximum allocations: control 220 minutes, three workers 100 each, trusted discovery 15, shadow aggregate 10 and authorization 5: 550 allocated runner-minutes. Existing source and mandatory aggregation jobs can add 10 minutes each, giving a 570 minute allocation ceiling for that enumerated graph, excluding other existing policy jobs and queue time. These job allocations are a ceiling, not expected consumption. The existing control workflow is not edited here; its actual approved settings and any additional jobs must be reconciled in the runnable exact-content review. Shadow critical-path allocation is 130 minutes (5 + 15 + 100 + 10); full comparison wall budget must accommodate the existing sequential gate and orchestration, proposed at 250 minutes excluding queueing. Stop after one pair regardless of outcome; request a new decision for another attempt.

Use job-scoped workspaces, basetemp, SQLite/database names, caches and native-library thread limits. Compare Python, dependency versions and thread settings across control/workers. Do not share mutable fixture state, ports or databases. Session fixtures run once per process; module ownership preserves within-module ordering only. Synthetic SQLite and mutable-global checks demonstrate the mechanism, not FINCO session, filesystem, financial, native BLAS or ordering safety. Hosted production evidence must assess those separately.

Cancel the comparison on source/manifest/environment drift, collection mismatch, unauthorized omission/overlap, lineage mismatch, budget exhaustion or external owner cancellation. Stop further scheduling, terminate only the job's descendant processes, close databases and retain failure evidence. Upload evidence even after test failure, with 14-day retention; cancellation uploads are best effort and missing artifacts mean failure. Tokens are read-only (`contents: read`; artifact consumer additionally `actions: read`), no secrets/write/deployment privileges. No automatic reruns, successful-marker overrides or merge-ready conclusion.
