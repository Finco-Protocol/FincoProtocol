# FINCO Protocol

**Financial Intelligence Infrastructure**

FINCO Protocol is building infrastructure for modelling, monitoring, comparing, simulating, and verifying financial assets across traditional and tokenized markets.

## Product surfaces

### FINCO Model
Deterministic financial modelling and scenario intelligence for real assets and structured finance.

### FINCO Radar
Reference-aware intelligence for tokenized markets, with execution-aware pricing, liquidity context, evidence, and deterministic market-state signals.

## Principles

- AI explains. FINCO calculates.
- Deterministic calculations over opaque scoring.
- Evidence and provenance over unsupported outputs.
- Fail closed when data authority is unavailable.
- Clean separation between modelling logic and market-data intelligence.
